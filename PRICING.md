# Prompt Builder – Pricing

Annual licenses. Updates are included while the license is valid.

| Edition | For | Activations | Price per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Evaluation** | Trying Prompt Builder | 1 | **Free** – 14 days |
| **Basic** | Individuals, students | 1 | **€ 19** incl. VAT |
| **Professional** | Teachers, trainers, freelancers | 3 | **€ 59** excl. VAT |
| **Team** | Companies and teams | 15 (10 users) | **€ 249** excl. VAT |
| **Team – Education** | Schools and training providers | 30 workstations | **€ 149** excl. VAT |

- **Basic** includes building prompts, the template library, your own templates, copy and export.
- **Professional and Team** add the Network / Cisco form, the SOC and Elastic form and sending prompts to an AI service.
- **Evaluation** has all features. Copied and exported text carries a small watermark.
- Local models through Ollama or LM Studio are free. A subscription with an online AI service is not included.

- the edition you want

You will receive your license key (`PRB-…`). Schools and organizations can order on invoice.
For a free 14-day Evaluation license, just ask by e-mail.
